WA Manager

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

WA Manager is a Chrome extension designed to help users organize and manage their WhatsApp Web communication, leads, message templates, scheduled messages and chat exports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is used, where it is stored and how users maintain control over their information.

1. Information We Access

When you use WA Manager on WhatsApp Web, the extension may access the following information visible within your WhatsApp Web session:

  • Phone numbers and labels displayed in your chat list.
  • Chat names and currently selected chat information.
  • Unread message indicators and last-message direction, to identify unattended conversations.
  • Messages currently loaded in an open chat when you explicitly use the chat export feature.
  • Message text and recipient information that you enter into templates, scheduler or broadcast tools.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by WA Manager is used only to provide its core features, including:

  • Identifying and organizing unsaved phone numbers.
  • Filtering unread and unattended leads.
  • Saving and managing reusable message templates.
  • Managing scheduled messages and broadcast queues.
  • Exporting messages from an open chat when requested.
  • Providing privacy blur controls for the WhatsApp Web interface.

3. Local Data Storage

WA Manager uses Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) to save extension data on your device.

This may include:

  • Collected lead lists and selection information.
  • Saved message templates.
  • Scheduled messages.
  • Broadcast recipient queues, message content and settings.
  • Your extension preferences.

This information is stored locally in your browser. WA Manager does not operate its own cloud storage or synchronization service.

4. Data Sharing and Selling

WA Manager does not sell, rent or share collected personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing.

The extension does not transmit collected lead lists, templates, scheduled messages or exported chat data to any developer-controlled external server.

When you use messaging features, WA Manager interacts with WhatsApp Web to prepare and send messages to recipients selected by you. Those communications are handled through WhatsApp and are subject to WhatsApp's own policies.

5. Chat Export

The chat export feature reads messages currently loaded in the open WhatsApp chat and creates a TXT file for download on your device.

Exported files are controlled by you. WA Manager does not upload these files to its servers.

6. Permissions

WA Manager requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • Storage: Used to save extension settings, leads, templates, scheduled messages and broadcast data locally in your browser.

The extension operates on WhatsApp Web (https://web.whatsapp.com/) to provide its features.

7. Data Security and User Control

Your extension data remains in Chrome's local storage. You can delete saved leads, templates and scheduled messages through the extension interface.

Uninstalling the extension or clearing its browser storage may remove locally stored extension data.

8. Children's Privacy

WA Manager is a general productivity tool and is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

9. Third-Party Services

WA Manager works with WhatsApp Web, a service operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. WA Manager is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

Your use of WhatsApp is subject to the applicable WhatsApp terms and privacy policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension's features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date.
